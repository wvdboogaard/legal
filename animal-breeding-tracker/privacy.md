# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: May 12, 2026**

Wim van den Boogaard ("we", "us", or "our") operates the **Animal Breeding Tracker** mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you.

### Types of Data Collected
- **Personal Data**: While using our Service, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you, such as your **email address**.
- **Animal Data**: We store data you enter about your animals (names, species, breeding dates, photos) to provide the tracking functionality.
- **Photos/Media**: If you choose to upload photos of your animals, we require access to your device's camera and photo library.

## 2. Service Providers
We use **Firebase (a Google service)** to provide:
- **Authentication**: To manage user accounts and logins.
- **Firestore Database**: To store and sync your breeding data across devices.
- **Storage**: To store animal photos.

Google's privacy policy applies to how they handle this data.

## 3. Data Retention
We will retain your Personal Data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy (i.e., as long as your account is active).

## 4. Security
The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 5. Children's Privacy
Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
